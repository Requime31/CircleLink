# Safety illustration assets

`BlockUserIllustration`, `BlockedPeopleEmptyIllustration`, and
`DeleteAccountIllustration` share one Sunset Parchment editorial style. Each imageset has
independent 256 px, 512 px, and 768 px PNG renditions for 1x, 2x, and 3x.

The PNGs use genuine transparency and intentionally have no separate light/dark
appearances: warm cream highlights remain visible on dark surfaces while clay and warm-ink
shapes retain contrast on the parchment canvas. Keep them aspect-fit at 160–240 pt and do
not template-render or tint them.
