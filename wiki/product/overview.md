# Product Overview

CircleLink is an iOS community messenger for discovering interest-based communities, meeting
people through Connect, and continuing relationships in direct or group chat. It supports iOS 16
and later and combines SwiftUI feature screens with a UIKit chat thread.

## Product surface

- Authentication: Apple and email authentication, age confirmation, and profile setup.
- Communities: discovery, search, create and edit, membership, posts, media, member lists, and
  group chat.
- Connect: candidate discovery, pass and say-hi gestures, incoming and outgoing requests,
  matches, direct-chat entry, reporting, and blocking.
- Chats: visible and hidden chats, pinning and reordering, mute, search, media, chat information,
  and direct or group conversations.
- Profile and settings: profile editing, profile posts, appearance, reminders, notifications,
  legal/help/rating, blocked people, and reversible account deactivation.
- Post activity: shared likes for community and profile posts and notification deep links to the
  referenced post.

Tasks, bugs, and roadmap state live in GitHub. This page records stable product scope.
